Capture Probe

Test page for the FieldSight phone app. Run each test in order, then tap Copy results and paste them back to Claude. Nothing is uploaded.

0. Device

1. Video format

Records two 5-second clips back to back, like the device's segments. Needs MP4.

2. Audio as WAV

Records 10 s of raw microphone audio and writes a 16 kHz mono WAV, same as the device. Speak normally.

3. Photo resolution

Grabs a photo from the in-page camera at the highest resolution the browser offers.

4. Does recording survive a photo?

Starts audio recording. While it runs: (a) tap In-page photo; then (b) tap System camera, take a photo there and come back. Then tap Stop. Reports any gap in the audio.
